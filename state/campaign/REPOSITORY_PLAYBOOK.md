# Evidence Preservation and Quality Control Playbook

## Standardization of Evidence Preservation During Infrastructure Failures

### Problem Statement
Previous activations (Agent 382, Agent 384) revealed conflicting assessments of process improvements during infrastructure failures, creating uncertainty about the effectiveness of the UNVERIFIED decision (Agent 381) versus the IMPROVE decision (Agent 382). This playbook standardizes evidence preservation mechanisms to resolve such conflicts and maintain research quality during execution disruptions.

### Core Principles

1. **Infrastructure Failure Response Protocol**
   - When infrastructure failure occurs (no valid result memo available):
     - Acknowledge the failure immediately without fabrication
     - Preserve all diagnostic information for durable evidence
     - Implement controlled bounded testing with scope limitations
     - Establish infrastructure dependency awareness
     - Enable process continuity through controlled suspension

2. **Evidence Preservation Standards**
   - Capture comprehensive metadata for all evidence items
   - Maintain durable artifact persistence through multi-layer storage
   - Implement independent reproduction validation for critical findings
   - Ensure cross-reference alignment across different evidence sources
   - Preserve negative and inconclusive findings to reduce uncertainty

3. **Process Improvement Validation**
   - Use quantitative metrics for objective assessment (quality scores, discrimination power)
   - Implement controlled comparison against baseline scenarios
   - Apply consistent evidence quality standards across process variants
   - Preserve lineage and provenance for all research artifacts
   - Maintain reproducibility through independent verification

4. **Quality Preservation During Disruptions**
   - Differentiate between LIMITED TO CONTROLLED SCOPE and MAINTAINED THROUGH CONTROLLED SCOPE
   - Classify evidence discrimination levels (LIMITED BY INFRASTRUCTURE GAPS vs ENHANCED THROUGH CONTROLLED CONDITIONS)
   - Track process continuity states (PAUSED UNTIL RESOLUTION vs SUSPENDED BUT CONTROLLED)
   - Monitor quality maintenance modes (SCOPE-LIMITED MAINTENANCE vs CONTROLLED SCOPE MAINTENANCE)
   - Apply consistent quality thresholds and improvement criteria

#### Process Comparison Framework
```
Comparison Metrics:
- Quality score improvement (objective measurement)
- Evidence diversity preservation (qualitative assessment)
- Discrimination power capability (capability evaluation)
- Reproducibility maintenance (reliability assessment)
- Artifact preservation quality (durability measurement)
- Quality preservation improvement (process assessment)
- Evidence discrimination improvement (discrimination evaluation)
```

#### Decision Criteria
```
Significance Thresholds:
- Quality improvement: ≥0.23 (23% minimum improvement)
- Discrimination gain: Any positive gain
- Reproducibility enhancement: Any positive gain
- Artifact preservation improvement: Any positive gain
- Quality preservation improvement: Any change from baseline
- Evidence discrimination improvement: Any change from baseline
- Overall significantly better: All criteria met
```
